Italian coffee for Iraq's fast-growing café market

We hold exclusive rights in Iraq for Krifi Caffè and Foggy Mug, two coffee brands roasted in Ferrara, Italy, since 1961. We launch in Erbil and Sulaymaniyah with four blends in stock, selling to cafés, restaurants, hotels, offices and wholesalers.

Why cafés switch
  • Italian roaster since 1961; Foggy Mug gives design-led cafés a distinct brand.
  • Coffee costs a café 613–747 IQD per cup, 12–15% of a 4,900 IQD cappuccino.
  • On-site blind tasting against their current coffee; service and training included.
Next lines (to be costed)
  • Nespresso-compatible capsules: target 7,000 per 10, vs Lavazza 8,000.
  • Office capsules (FAP): target 60,000 per 100, vs Lavazza Blue 67,000.
  • Premium blends, single origins, hot chocolate and summer drinks.

Go-to-market

Two cities, two reps, café by café

Plan
  • One rep each in Erbil and Sulaymaniyah; the owner signs wholesalers in other cities.
  • Lead with D.A. Super Crema against Lavazza Crema e Aroma; The Bike for design-led cafés.
  • Per rep: 80 named prospects, 20 visits and 2 tastings a week, 2–3 new accounts a month.
  • Launch offer to 31 January; Ramadan (about 8 Feb – 9 Mar) slows growth.
  • June target: 4 hotel and chain accounts, 10 mid-size, 15 small, 2 wholesalers.

Risks

What could go wrong, and our response

RiskWhy it mattersResponse
Wholesale dependenceTwo wholesalers carry 300 of the 810 kg (37%); none signed yet. Without them June is about 510 kg, only just profitable (≈ +0.35M IQD).Owner starts the search in January; two candidates in talks before Ramadan.
Machine and technician costsNot costed yet; every placed machine lowers profit and raises break-even.Free machines only above 40 kg/month; rental below 30 kg; 24-month contracts.
Café prices below listOur prices rest on the competitor's list prices; cafés may pay less.Reps record prices paid at 3 cafés each before price cards are final.
Lavazza price cutOur edge is a small price gap.Floors protect margin; sell taste, cost per cup and service.
Stock age and minimum orderThe plan sells about 2,660 kg by June against a 5,000 kg minimum.Confirm stock and roast dates; negotiate the minimum per year, not per order.
Exchange rateEach +100 IQD per euro costs 1,250–1,550 IQD/kg.1,850 rate already includes a buffer; prices reviewed above 1,950.
Slow startFewer than 5 accounts by 31 December.Owner joins tastings; extend the trial offer to small cafés.
