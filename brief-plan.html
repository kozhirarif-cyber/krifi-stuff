  • Lavazza's best-selling café blends are only 40–50% Arabica.
  • Our coffee costs a café 613–747 IQD per cup, about 12–15% of a 4,900 IQD cappuccino.

Go-to-market

Two cities, two reps, café by café

Plan
  • One rep each in Erbil and Sulaymaniyah; owner signs wholesalers in other cities.
  • Start with independent cafés now using Lavazza; Foggy Mug for design-led cafés.
  • 20 visits and 2 tastings per rep a week; about 3 new accounts a month each.
  • June target: 4 hotel and chain accounts, 10 mid-size, 15 small, 2 wholesalers.
Key risks and responses
  • Brand unknown: blind tastings, cost per cup, service package.
  • Lavazza price cut: price floors protect margin; lead with value blends.
  • Exchange rate: prices reviewed when the euro passes 1,950 IQD.
  • Stock and cash: negotiate the 5,000 kg minimum over 12 months.
